# Grocer-Eaze Product Backlog

## Recipe provider expansion

- Evaluate FatSecret Platform API as the next low-cost recipe source. Confirm current pricing, attribution, caching, image, dietary-filter, and production-use terms before implementation.
- Re-evaluate other free or inexpensive recipe providers as their plans change, with priority given to complete ingredient quantities, source URLs, images, pagination, and gluten-free/low-dairy/Mediterranean filtering.
- Keep provider results normalized behind the existing recipe catalog so sorting, filtering, deduplication, grocery aggregation, and source attribution remain consistent.
- Do not add a provider until its quota behavior has a graceful fallback and its license permits the required display and caching behavior.
